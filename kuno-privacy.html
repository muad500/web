Privacy Policy

Kuno Card Party - Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Kuno Card Party ("Kuno", "we", "us") collects when you use the app, and how it is used.

Information We Collect

  • Account information - your email address and/or username, and a unique player ID, when you register an account or sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Gameplay data - match results, experience points, level, and in-game statistics tied to your player ID.
  • Voice chat audio - transmitted live between players during a multiplayer match. This audio is not recorded or stored by us; it exists only for the duration of the live match.
  • Device and diagnostic information - basic technical data (device model, OS version, crash logs) used to keep the app stable and fix bugs.

How We Use This Information

  • To operate multiplayer matches, matchmaking, and voice chat
  • To save and display your profile, level, and leaderboard position
  • To authenticate you when signing in with Google or Apple
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or tracking.

Third-Party Services

Kuno uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • PlayFab (Microsoft) - account authentication and player data storage
  • Google Sign-In - optional sign-in method
  • Sign in with Apple - optional sign-in method

These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app: Settings > Account > Delete Account. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

Kuno is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
