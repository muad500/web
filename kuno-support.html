Email us at syedmouad@gmail.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can. Please include your device (iPhone/Android model), what you were doing, and a screenshot if you have one - it helps a lot.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign in?

You can play as a guest right away, or sign in with your Google or Apple account from the login screen to save your progress across devices.

Voice chat isn't working / I can't hear other players

Check that Kuno has microphone permission in your device's Settings app. You can also mute or unmute yourself at any time by tapping your own avatar during a match.

How do I mute another player?

Tap that player's avatar during a match and choose Mute.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings > Account, then tap Delete Account. You'll be asked to confirm twice - this permanently deletes your account and progress and cannot be undone.

I got disconnected mid-match. What happens to my progress?

You can usually rejoin the same match if you reconnect quickly. If the match ends while you're disconnected, let us know at the email above with the approximate time it happened.

Privacy

Kuno collects your account email/username and in-game identifiers to run multiplayer matches and save your progress. Voice chat audio is transmitted live between players during a match and is not recorded or stored. You can request account deletion at any time in-app.
